Privacy Policy

Health Dashboard for iPhone and Apple Watch · Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: your health data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in any device backups you make. Health Dashboard has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

Who we are

Health Dashboard is made by Ashley Libasci, an individual developer. Questions about this policy: healthdashboard.help@gmail.com.

What the app reads, and why

With your permission, the app reads from Apple Health to work out your sleep need, best wake time, energy curve, recovery, stress, cycle, fitness and related insights:

  • Sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, wrist temperature and blood pressure
  • Time in daylight, steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, workouts and their distances, VO₂ max and weight
  • Caffeine and water you've logged in Health
  • Your birthday (to use your age), cycle tracking data and, if you turn it on, medication doses you've logged
  • Moods from Apple's State of Mind log

All of this is processed on your device. You can change what the app may read at any time in the Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices.

What the app saves to Apple Health

Caffeine, water and weight you log, and strength and Pilates workouts you log on your Apple Watch (with their heart rate and energy). If you turn it on, bleeding, symptoms and your birth control method, so they're kept if you reinstall. Turning that off leaves what's already saved in Health.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your settings and logs (check-ins, drinks, water, cycle logs, exams and similar) are stored on your iPhone and shared with your Apple Watch and widgets. Like other app data, this is included in device backups you make to iCloud or your computer.

Optional features

  • Apple Calendar: if you connect it, the app looks through the calendars on your iPhone for exams (events with words like "exam", "midterm" or "quiz" in the title) so it can plan your sleep around them. It only reads your calendar, never changes it, and your events are never sent to us. Exams it finds are saved with your other logs.
  • Location: with the Sky theme, your approximate (city-level) location is used on your phone to work out sunrise and sunset. It's never sent anywhere.
  • Motion (Apple Watch): the smart alarm uses movement during your wake window to wake you as you start stirring.
  • Alarms and notifications: reminders and the backup alarm are scheduled on your device.

Sharing

The app doesn't sell, share or send your data to anyone, and nothing is ever sent to us. Your data is saved on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in your device backups if you use them. Otherwise it leaves your device only when you choose to export your data or a visit summary through the share sheet. Links to outside health websites open in your browser.

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. On the App Store, Apple shares crash and usage information with developers only if you've opted in to that in your iPhone's settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings has an option that removes everything the app saved on your phone, Apple Watch and widgets. Deleting the app does the same. Data saved to Apple Health stays in Health until you delete it there, but older caffeine, water and bleeding from Health won't come back into the app. Device backups you already made keep their copy until they're replaced.

Health information

Health Dashboard gives general wellness information. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.

Children

The app isn't intended for children under 13.
